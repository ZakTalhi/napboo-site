Delete your Napboo account

In the app

  1. Open Napboo. On Home, tap the Profile button at the top of the screen.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password, or confirm with Google if that's how you sign in, then tap Delete forever.

If you own a family that has other members, Napboo tells you who will become its owner. To choose someone else, hand the family on before you delete your account.

Without the app

Email support@napboo.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Napboo account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account (your email address, name, password and time zone), your sessions, your plan and your membership of every family. It is permanent and can't be undone.

A family belongs to everyone in it. If you were a family's last member, the family and everything in it (its babies, entries and history) are deleted with your account. If other members remain, the entries you logged stay in that family's log, without your name.

Signing out or deleting your account also removes your Napboo data from that phone.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first: on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, and on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. The store keeps a record that the purchase happened.
